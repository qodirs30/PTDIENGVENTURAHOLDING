# MASTER PROMPT 01 — PRODUCT, BRAND & INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

## Project
Build the official investor-facing landing page for **PT Dieng Ventura Holdings**.

The website must synthesize FILE 01–FILE 07:
1. Vision
2. Holding Company
3. Investment Proposal
4. Financial Model
5. Investor Pitch Deck
6. Investor Valuation & Term Sheet
7. Investor Data Room

## Primary objective
Position PT Dieng Ventura Holdings as a **premium, credible, investor-ready Tourism & Investment Holding Company** building an integrated tourism, nature, community and investment ecosystem from Dieng, Central Java.

This is NOT a generic tourism website and NOT a brochure. It is a corporate/investment landing page designed to:
- communicate the investment thesis clearly;
- explain the holding-company model;
- introduce the flagship project KARANGSARI FOREST PARK;
- demonstrate scalable business architecture;
- establish trust and corporate credibility;
- generate qualified investor/strategic-partner inquiries;
- provide controlled access to deeper investor materials.

## Content rules
- Use concise, executive-level copy.
- Avoid hype.
- Avoid guaranteed-return language.
- Use “potential”, “projected”, “target”, and “indicative” where appropriate.
- Do not claim ownership, permits, contracts, valuations, investors or regulatory approvals without verified documentation.
- Do not publicly expose confidential term sheets, cap tables or sensitive financial/legal documents.
- Every numeric claim must have a source.
- Every legal/investment claim must be reviewed before production.

## UX principles
- Premium and calm.
- Strong visual hierarchy.
- One major message per section.
- Clear CTA hierarchy.
- Mobile-first.
- Fast scanning for investors.
- High trust, low clutter.
- Use real project imagery only when approved; otherwise use clearly marked placeholders.
